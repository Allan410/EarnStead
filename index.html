A realistic process

Turn a listing into a real opportunity.

Do not spend weeks collecting links. Pick one route, make proof of skill, and contact real buyers consistently.

1

Choose one lane

Examples: basic websites, poster design, tutoring, data cleanup, or a paid internship related to your course. Avoid trying five paths at once.

2

Show proof

Create 2–3 honest samples. Label practice projects as samples; never invent clients, testimonials, certificates or results.

3

Agree in writing

Confirm deliverables, deadline, revisions, payment and ownership before starting. Use platform payment protections when available.

Protect your time and money

Legitimate work should survive basic questions.

Scammers often sell certainty: guaranteed daily income, easy tasks, or urgent deposits. Treat these as warning signs—not as a shortcut.

Rule of thumb: If the business model is unclear and you must pay first to receive work, walk away and verify independently.

✕
Pay a registration, security or training fee to get hiredDo not pay a recruiter to release a job, interview, salary or task balance. Verify any training course separately.
✕
“Guaranteed ₹X per day” for trivial workIncome depends on skill, demand, competition, hours and clients. No honest directory can guarantee your earnings.
✕
Deposit money to complete tasks or withdraw earningsDo not transfer money or crypto to “recharge” a task account. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs or remote access.
✕
Unclear employer identity or copied job descriptionCheck the company’s official domain, contact details and role. Search the wording independently before sharing documents.
✓
Use official resources to verifyIndia’s National Career Service says its registration and job application services are free. Official site: ncs.gov.in.
First-customer plan

Four weeks. One clear offer.

This is a work plan, not an earnings forecast. Your first paid project may take longer—or may not happen until you improve the offer.

Week 1

Make one offer

Pick a service you can deliver now. Build two demo samples and a one-page portfolio using free hosting.

Week 2

Find real buyers

List 30 relevant prospects: local tutors, shops, clubs, small organisations or creators whose needs you understand.

Week 3

Personal outreach

Send 5 tailored messages per weekday. Show one useful observation and a sample; do not spam or promise impossible results.

Week 4

Close and learn

Offer a small, fixed-scope paid pilot. Write down scope, price and deadline. If nobody responds, improve the offer—not the income claim.

Pricing starter idea (not a market-rate guarantee): A very small static website or poster pack might be offered at an introductory fixed price after checking local demand and the work involved. Quote only after defining scope. Do not promise custom software, unlimited revisions, SEO rankings or business results you cannot control.
